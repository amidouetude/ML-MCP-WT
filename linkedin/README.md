## Posting notes

- LinkedIn crops long posts after ~3 lines behind a "see more" — the first
  1-2 lines in each draft are written as the hook, keep them intact.
- Attach one figure (PNG, already in `paper/figures/`) rather than a wall of
  text; posts with an image consistently outperform text-only ones.
- Native-upload the PDF (`paper/main.pdf`) as a document if you want people to
  read the full paper without leaving LinkedIn — outbound links get
  deprioritized by LinkedIn's feed ranking, so prefer document upload or
  "link in comments" over a link in the post body.
- Suggested cadence: post 2 (headline result) first since it's the easiest
  scroll-stopper, then post 1 (story) a few days later once the first has
  landed, then post 3 (lessons) to invite engineering discussion/comments.
- Tag/mention your university or lab if applicable, and consider `#MPC
  #WindEnergy #MachineLearning #ControlSystems #MATLAB` as hashtags — pick 3-5,
  not all of them.
